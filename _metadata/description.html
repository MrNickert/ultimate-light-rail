Build your city's light rail network. Surface, underground or elevated. Through stations or reversing termini, with two tracks and platforms of 40, 60, 80, 100 or 120 metres. Fences are optional above ground. Open the Light Rail construction menu between Road and Rail. It brings together the tram depot, fenced light rail track and configurable stations. Connect native electric tram track and assign your existing tram vehicles. Stations and fenced track become available in 1925; depot availability follows the base game. Terminus stations include a scissors crossover. Through stations can add a crossover at each end with Turnback. Vehicles reverse without a turning loop. Use vehicles that fit the platform, and select terminals or waypoints when a specific track assignment is needed. Build an Underground Interchange with one to four layers. Choose a platform length of 40–120 m and an angle of 0°, 45°, 90° or 135° per layer. Each layer has Turnback crossovers. A shared street entrance and internal stairs connect all platforms. FOR STATION CREATORS Station API v1 provides a shared menu and station logic for your own visual components. Ordinary platform and canopy modules use a 20m grid. The core owns entrances, stairs, routing and the two-track layout. Updates within the documented v1 contract remain backward compatible; breaking changes get a new API version alongside v1. Documentation and developer kit: https://github.com/MrNickert/ultimate-light-rail Keep this mod and required asset packs installed while their stations are used in a save.
