Ultimate Light Rail by MrNickert.
Transport Fever 3 and the referenced native game assets are by Urban Games.
The hidden lightrail_double.street_template.lua is derived from Urban Games tram_new.street_template.lua with additional transport modes.
The station references the native small_new shelter, tram street style, shared ground textures and bare single_rail visual model. All models/materials/textures are loaded from the installed game and are not redistributed.

Underground entrance layout data adapted from Urban Games underground_station.script.lua. Underground meshes, textures, construction helpers and pedestrian circulation are referenced from the installed game, not redistributed.
Menu illustrations generated with OpenAI image generation; illustrative previews, not game screenshots.

Surface platform visual wrapper references Urban Games rail/era_c/perron_5.mdl LOD meshes and materials with transport metadata removed. Low platforms, plaza, paving, benches, bins, planters, lights and tram poles reference installed game assets. Decorative contact wires reuse the installed bare rail mesh at a thin scale. No mesh/material/texture binaries from the game are redistributed.

0.8.4: Native perron_10_head_con meshes/materials are referenced for the head plaza. Native apple and azalea models provide plaza greenery. The tram ribbon mesh is original generated geometry; only its material/texture is referenced from Urban Games new_medium_tram_track. No native mesh/texture binary is redistributed.

0.8.5: Native new_medium_tram_paving material is referenced beneath the tram rail texture, using the same original strip geometry. No additional native binaries are redistributed.

0.9.0: native perron_5_underpass LOD-meshes, materials and lighting are referenced by a visual-only wrapper. Passenger curves are adapted from Urban Games addon_platform_passenger_stairs_era_c.module.lua. Terrain openings reference the native hole.gtex. No native binary assets are redistributed.

0.10.0: the public fenced track preserves Urban Games tram_new traffic/layout data. Native industrial fence_metal_4m meshes/materials (uniform scale 0.4) are referenced by a visual-only edge wrapper, not redistributed.

0.10.1: fenced-track menu illustration generated with OpenAI image generation; illustrative preview, not an in-game screenshot.

0.13.0: elevated viaduct, foundations and 82 stair treads reuse native concrete bridge models (c_6_rep_rep, c_6_rep_l/r, c_pillar_small_bot_m) with project-defined assembly/transforms and pedestrian paths. No native mesh or texture binaries are redistributed.

0.13.1 supersedes the loose skinned bridge models: original closed concrete geometry references Urban Games c_concrete_unskinned material/textures. Native shelter LOD references are wrapped as a collision-free entrance visual. No native mesh/texture binaries are redistributed.
