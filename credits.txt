Ultimate Metro by MrNickert.
Transport Fever 3 and the referenced native game assets are by Urban Games.
The hidden lightrail_double.street_template.lua is derived from Urban Games tram_new.street_template.lua with additional transport modes.
The station references the native small_new shelter, tram street style, shared ground textures and bare single_rail visual model. All models/materials/textures are loaded from the installed game and are not redistributed.

Underground entrance layout data adapted from Urban Games underground_station.script.lua. Underground meshes, textures, construction helpers and pedestrian circulation are referenced from the installed game, not redistributed.
Menu illustrations generated with OpenAI image generation; illustrative previews, not game screenshots.

Surface platform visual wrapper references Urban Games rail/era_c/perron_5.mdl LOD meshes and materials with transport metadata removed. Low platforms, plaza, paving, benches, bins, planters, lights and tram poles reference installed game assets. Decorative contact wires reuse the installed bare rail mesh at a thin scale. No mesh/material/texture binaries from the game are redistributed.
