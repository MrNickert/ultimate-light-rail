All final gallery images must be real captures from the current release in Transport Fever 3. Keep scenery, station geometry, vehicles and passengers exactly as captured. Use the supplied promotional references for colour and typography only.

The first four provide the minimum useful launch gallery. All six give a stronger overview. Do not rebuild a city solely for these shots; an operating line in an existing save is useful evidence of normal gameplay.

## Capture settings

- Prefer original PNG captures at 1920×1080 or higher, landscape 16:9. These are our production targets, not a claim about the platform's minimum requirements.
- Hide the HUD for scenic shots; retain it for the settings shot.
- Move the pointer off the subject and avoid selected-building highlights, collision overlays and floating construction costs.
- Use consistent daylight where possible. Include at least one tram at a platform; passengers are useful if naturally present.
- Send originals without title text, sharpening or filters. Branding is added separately.
- A short optional clip of a tram stopping, changing ends and departing through the crossover would demonstrate reversing particularly well.

## Validation captures, separate from the gallery

The 1924/1925 availability check is a test result rather than a promotional screenshot. Record whether stations/track become available automatically in 1925 or require a reload. Do not mark that check complete until confirmed.
