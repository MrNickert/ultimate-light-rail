## Capture settings

- Prefer original PNG captures at 1920×1080 or higher, landscape 16:9. These are our production targets, not a claim about the platform's minimum requirements.
- Hide the HUD for scenic shots; retain it for the settings shot.
- Move the pointer off the subject and avoid selected-building highlights, collision overlays and floating construction costs.
- Use consistent daylight where possible. Include at least one tram at a platform; passengers are useful if naturally present.
- Send originals without title text, sharpening or filters. Branding is added separately.
- A short optional clip of a tram stopping, changing ends and departing through the crossover would demonstrate reversing particularly well.
