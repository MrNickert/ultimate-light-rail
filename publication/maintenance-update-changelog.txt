Underground Ultimate Light Rail stations now use the same maintenance base as rebalanced vanilla underground stops, without requiring the separate cost mod. Existing player-owned bundled underground stations using the previous default are adjusted once. Elevated stations now cost 20% more to maintain than surface stations. Existing player-owned bundled elevated stations using the old default are adjusted once. Construction prices and station layouts are unchanged.
The Light Rail station and track have new artwork in the construction menu.
